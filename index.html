Interactive UX Case Study

Arrear Clearing — Student Academic Success App

My Role
Lead UI/UX Designer
Duration
6 Weeks Sprint
Primary Tool
Figma & FigJam
Target Users
Diploma & Engg Students

The Problem

Engineering and diploma students struggling with backlog subjects face immense stress due to scattered study notes, missing previous-year question papers, confusing syllabus updates, and an inability to find reliable mentor support before exams.

The UX Solution

Created an all-in-one mobile companion providing direct access to subject-wise curated notes, indexed past-year question papers with solutions, an interactive progress tracker, and a mentor booking feature for 1-on-1 guidance.

Primary User Persona: Karthik R.

Demographics & Habits

• 3rd Year Diploma in Computer Science
• 2 backlog papers (Data Structures, Math III)
• Studies mostly late at night via smartphone
• Gets overwhelmed by lengthy textbook PDFs

Key Needs & Frustrations

• Needs solved question papers from the past 5 years
• Requires quick 15-minute doubt-clearing sessions
• Needs visual encouragement and progress analytics
• Dark mode to prevent eye strain during late nights

Screen Architecture Preview

Screen 1: Backlog Dashboard & Subject Cards

Clean vertical list of student backlogs (e.g. Data Structures, Algorithms, DBMS). Each card displays syllabus progress percentage, quick action buttons for Notes, Video Lessons, and Previous Year Questions, and exam date countdown pill.

Screen 2: Searchable Previous-Year Question Papers (PYQP)

Enables students to filter past papers by examination session (May 2023, Dec 2022, May 2022). Each paper card highlights recurring high-frequency questions, 1-tap PDF download, and solution keys verified by college faculty.

Screen 3: Mentor Connect & 1-on-1 Guidance

Connects students directly to senior peer tutors and professors for scheduled 15-minute voice/chat sessions to review difficult derivations and exam-clearing strategies.

Screen 4: Completion Analytics & Study Streak

Motivational bar graphs and completion rings showing daily study hours logged, solved PYQP papers count, and mock exam readiness score.
